# Code of conduct

ATLAS follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and keep discussion about the
work. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not tolerated in issues,
pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through the contact on the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/urveesh09). Reports are handled
confidentially. The maintainer may remove content, or block or ban participants, following
the enforcement guidelines of the Covenant.
